## Why visit Varendra Research Museum?

Make space in your Rajshahi trip for the region’s history. Varendra Research Museum brings together archaeological collections that help visitors explore Bengal’s cultural past. It is a thoughtful stop for people interested in heritage, and a different pace from a day spent moving between outdoor destinations.

The museum and Varendra Research Society were established in **1910** through the efforts of **Kumar Sarat Kumar Ray, Akshaya Kumar Maitreya and Ramaprasad Chanda**, according to the University of Rajshahi’s institutional history.

## Plan your visit before setting out

Confirm current arrangements with the museum before fixing your itinerary. We arrange transport; museum admission and access remain subject to the museum’s own rules.

- **Opening hours and closures:** check the schedule for your travel date, especially around holidays.
- **Tickets:** confirm current entry fees and any separate visitor categories.
- **Photography and bags:** ask staff what is permitted before taking pictures or entering with luggage.
- **Accessibility:** if someone in your group has mobility needs, confirm access arrangements directly with the museum.

See the [University of Rajshahi’s museum information](https://www.ru.ac.bd/facilities/varendra-research-museum/) as a starting point. Avoid planning around an old ticket price or an unconfirmed timetable.

## A relaxed city-day itinerary

This is a suggested order, not a timed package. Adjust it to the museum’s confirmed hours, the weather and your group’s pace.

1. **Start with the museum.** Leave enough time to explore without rushing to the next stop.
2. **Take a lunch break.** Choose somewhere convenient for your group or return to your hotel.
3. **Add an optional city stop.** Discuss where you would like to go and any access restrictions in advance.
4. **Finish near the Padma.** If weather and timing allow, include a riverside stop before returning.

Agree the stops and return time with us before departure. Additional destinations can affect the quote.
