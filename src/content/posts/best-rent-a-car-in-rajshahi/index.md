## Top Destinations Covered by Our Drivers

### Varendra Research Museum

Bangladesh’s oldest museum (est. 1910) houses 9,000+ artefacts. Located in Rajshahi city centre — 5 minutes from our depot. Most visitors spend 2–3 hours here. Best visited weekday mornings to avoid school groups.

### Puthia Temple Complex

The largest collection of Hindu temples in Bangladesh — 23 temples dating from the 17th–19th century, 32 km east of Rajshahi. The Govinda and Shiva temples are particularly photogenic at sunrise. Our drivers know the access roads and best photo spots.

### Padma River Embankment

Watch the sun set over the Padma from Rajshahi’s riverbank promenade. Street food stalls serve fresh hilsa and local snacks from dusk. A 10-minute drive from the city centre.

### Rajshahi Silk Trail

Visit working silk weaving workshops in Sareebar (25 km), the Rajshahi Silk Research Institute, and government silk emporia. Our drivers turn this into a 4–5 hour cultural half-day tour.

### Somapura Mahavihara, Paharpur (UNESCO World Heritage Site)

A 9th-century Buddhist monastery 75 km north — one of the greatest Buddhist viharas in South Asia and a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1985. Full-day trip from Rajshahi; best combined with a visit to the Paharpur Museum on site.

### Chapai Nawabganj Mango Country

In season (May–July), visit the mango orchards of Chapai Nawabganj — 40 km west of Rajshahi. Our drivers connect you with orchard owners for farm visits, fresh mango tasting, and direct purchases.
