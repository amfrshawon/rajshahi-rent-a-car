## Explore Rajshahi’s Silk City by Private Car – A Local Guide

Rajshahi is not merely a city—it’s the epicenter of **Bangladesh’s silk industry**, a living heritage spanning centuries. While many visitors focus on temples and monuments, the true character of Rajshahi emerges through its silk traditions, from weaving workshops where artisans practice techniques unchanged for generations, to bustling markets where merchants trade the finest materials. A private car rental allows you to explore this silk heritage authentically, moving between workshop to market to residential area seamlessly. This guide unveils Rajshahi’s identity as a silk capital.

### Rajshahi: Bangladesh’s Silk Capital

Rajshahi produces **over 60% of Bangladesh’s silk**, a distinction earned through centuries of expertise and ideal environmental conditions. The region’s warm, humid climate and fertile soil support mulberry cultivation—the foundation of silk production. Historically, Rajshahi silks were treasured across South Asia, and today the industry remains vital to local economy and culture.

Unlike other tourist destinations, Rajshahi’s silk heritage isn’t confined to museums. It’s woven into the city’s daily fabric. Wearing a Rajshahi silk saree is a statement of cultural pride across Bangladesh. Understanding this heritage requires visiting the places where silk is actually produced and traded.

### The Silk Weaving Workshops at Sareebar

Approximately **25 kilometers from Rajshahi city**, the village of Sareebar is home to several hundred silk weaving families operating from home workshops. A rental car allows you to visit multiple workshops in a single morning, something impossible via public transport. These aren’t tourist attractions with fixed hours—they’re active working environments where weavers have practiced their craft since childhood.
