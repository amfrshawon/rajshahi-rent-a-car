The museum, located just five minutes from downtown Rajshahi, houses **over 9,000 artifacts** spanning centuries of Bengali history. Entry cost only **৳20**, and Karim waited in the lobby while Rakib spent two hours exploring Pala-period sculptures, ancient terracotta, and rare manuscripts. The 32-kilometer drive eastward took 45 minutes on well-maintained roads. Puthia’s **23 Hindu temples** sprawled across the countryside like ancient sentinels. Karim navigated the narrow village lanes confidently, parked near the Govinda Temple, and served as an informal guide. He explained the architectural differences between temples, shared historical anecdotes, and photographed Rakib against the ornate facades. Without Karim’s local knowledge, Rakib would have missed the smaller, equally spectacular Shiva Temple tucked behind the main complex.

“This car rental isn’t just about transportation,” Rakib found himself thinking. “It’s about access to authentic experience.”

As afternoon heat intensified, Karim guided the car toward the Padma River embankment—Rajshahi’s cultural heart.
