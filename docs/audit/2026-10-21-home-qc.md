## Home-page length

The audit's additional sections took the home page back to **5,461 px
(7.00 screens)** at 360 × 780 — the length the redesign had just removed.
Compacting every section without deleting audit content brings it to
**4,471 px (5.73 screens)**.

What changed:

- Hero, route board, fleet: tighter spacing; hero and fleet photos move from
  16:10 to 16:9.
- Packages: four large tiles become a compact two-column link list.
- Standard, ambulance, corporate, steps and area: smaller headings, tighter
  padding, one-line leads.
- The blog link moves into the service-area section instead of its own strip.
- Route-board rows keep the 44 px target at `min-h-11`.

**Decision:** the 3,500 px target in `docs/audit/REDESIGN-PROMPT.md` predates
the 2026-10-20 audit, which asks for six more sections on the home page
(ambulance, standard, packages, corporate, FAQ, area). Hitting 3,500 px would
mean deleting content the newer audit requests. All requested content is kept,
compacted; the length target is superseded for this home page.

## Transfer

Home transfer is **452 KiB**, against the earlier 400 KiB target. The two font
files account for 206 KiB: Noto Sans Bengali (105 KiB, preloaded) and Anek
Latin (101 KiB, loaded for Latin runs inside Bangla pages). The Bengali
preload is inside the 150 KiB budget. Dropping Anek Latin from Bangla pages
would save 101 KiB but change the Latin typography the redesign specified, so
it was left in place. Performance and LCP are unaffected by this overage.
